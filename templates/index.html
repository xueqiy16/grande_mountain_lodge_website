Who Are We?


Perched on the edge of the Canadian Rockies in the heart of Grande Cache, the Grande Mountain Lodge is a place to stay for nature enthusiasts! We're surrounded by the raw, untamed majesty of the Willmore Wilderness—one of Alberta’s best-kept secrets. Our motel lodge serves as a warm refuge after days spent navigating 2,000 km of mountain trails. We invite travelers to rediscover the spirit of the northern wilderness.

About Our Lodge

Grande Mountain Lodge aims to accomodate travelers seeking both adventure and comfort.

We offer single, double and family rooms with heating, air-conditioning, private bathrooms, a balcony and kitchenette. Guests enjoy free wifi and on-site parking, alongside an outdoor seating and picnic area. We look forward to being your home away from home in the heart of the mountains.

Our Location

Grande Mountain Lodge is located 1.4 km from Willmore Wilderness Park, surrounded by the untouched wilderness of the Canadian Rockies.

Our lodge is a short walk from the town center of Grande Cache, local shops, restaurants, and the Grande Cache Recreation Centre. Our guests are also steps away from the Sulphur Gates Canyon and the town’s vibrant Indigenous culture.

Whether you are here for the world-class hiking or the warm community spirit, we provide the ultimate basecamp for your adventure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Check-in is from 3:00 PM - 9:00 PM and Check-out is until 11:00 AM. You must show government-issued photo identification and a credit card upon Check-in. Please inform the lodge in advance of your expected arrival time with our contact information provided on this website and on the reservation confirmation received after booking.

The minimum Check-in age is 18 years old.

Cancellations made within 24 hours of check-in will be charged the first night's room rate. No-show: Guests who do not arrive and do not cancel their reservation will be charged the first night's room rate. If your plans change, we simply ask that you give us a quick call at 780-827-2007 or email reception@grandemountainlodge.com.

If you require early arrival or late departure, please email or phone us. A special request may be made while booking.

Note: Special requests cannot be guaranteed and may incur additional charges, but we will do our best to accommodate your needs based on availability.
