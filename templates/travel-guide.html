Grande Cache: Home of the Canadian Death Race

Grande Cache is famous for its "Passport to the Peaks" program. Conquer the surrounding summits including Mt. Hamell and Mt. Stearn for breathtaking 360-degree views.

Whether you want a leisurely walk to Sulphur Gates or a grueling summit hike, there is a trail for every skill level right outside our door.



Local Activities & Highlights

There is something for everyone at Grande Cache. Explore everything Grande Cache has to offer.

Wild Blue Yonder Rafting

Experience high-adrenaline white water rafting on the Smoky or Sulphur rivers for an unforgettable mountain thrill.

Ice Climbing

Grande Cache is known for ice climbing. If you have the confidence and fitness, try out this sport at Mount Hamel's Evergreen Gully or Grande Mountain's Knuckle-Gnasher Gully!

Tourism & Interpretive Centre

Discover dinosaur tracks from the Cretaceous period and immerse yourself in the rich local Indigenous history and culture.

Mountain Horseback Riding

Explore the rugged beauty of the Rockies on a guided horseback tour, offering a unique perspective of the surrounding wilderness.

Golf & Country Club

Tee off at the Grande Cache Golf and Country Club, featuring incredible mountain views from every hole on the course.

Fishing

Enjoy the pristine waters of Grande Cache, where you can fish for rainbow trout, northern pike and more! Grande Cache offers a novel of lakes, rivers and creeks for fishing!

Waterfalls

Grande Cache is home to stunning natural waterfalls. These sites offer visitors spectacular photography opportunities and are easily accessible just minutes from local accommodations.

Some of our most popular waterfalls include:

• Muskeg Falls
• Twin Falls
• Eaton Falls
• Torrens Falls
• Kakwa Lower Falls



Must-Visit Parks

Grande Cache's parks offer a diverse range of outdoor experiences, from peaceful meditation spaces to vast, untamed mountain wilderness. These protected areas serve as spectacular vantage points for visitors.

Grande Cache Labyrinth Park

This peaceful walking meditation park features an 11-circuit medieval-style labyrinth designed to help visitors unwind and de-stress against a beautiful mountain backdrop.

Sulphur Gates Canyon

One of the area's most popular attractions, this scenic destination features impressive sandstone cliffs and viewing platforms overlooking the dramatic confluence of the clear Sulphur and silty Smoky rivers.

Willmore Wilderness Park

Spanning approximately 4,600 square kilometers, this pure mountain wilderness offers a raw backcountry experience for seasoned hikers and horseback riders, featuring ancient glaciers, thick forests, and over 750 kilometers of untamed trails.
