General Policies
Cancellation policies

Reservations may be cancelled without charge until 24 hours (by 3:00 PM the day before arrival) before the scheduled check-in date. Cancellations made within 24 hours of check-in will be charged the first night's room rate (taxed). To cancel, you can click cancel in the confirmation email you receieve, give us a quick call at 780-827-2007 or email us at reception@grandemountainlodge.com.

No Show policies

Guests who do not arrive and do not cancel their reservation will be charged the first night's room rate (taxed). If your plans change, we simply ask that you give us a quick call at 780-827-2007 or email reception@grandemountainlodge.com.

Tax policies

A 5% Goods and Services Tax (GST) applies to taxable accommodation charges. A 6% Alberta Tourism Levy (ATL) applies to eligible accommodation booked on or after April 1, 2026; the levy does not apply when the same guest continuously occupies the lodging for 28 days or more.

Child policies

Children of all ages are welcome. Children 18 and above will be charged as adults at this property.

Crib and extra bed policies

No cribs or extra beds are available.
