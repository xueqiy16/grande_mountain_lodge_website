General Policies
Cancellation Policy

You can cancel for free until 1 day (24 hrs) before your arrival. If you cancel within 1 day of arrival, the cancellation fee will be the cost of the first night. If you don't show up, the no-show fee will be the same as the cancellation fee. You can use the link in the confirmation email to cancel your booking.

Cancellation deadlines are in the property's local time. (MST)

Tax policies

A 5% Goods and Services Tax (GST) applies to taxable accommodation charges. A 6% Alberta Tourism Levy (ATL) applies to eligible accommodation booked on or after April 1, 2026; the levy does not apply when the same guest continuously occupies the lodging for 28 days or more.

Child policies

Children of all ages are welcome. Children 18 and above will be charged as adults at this property.

Crib and extra bed policies

No cribs or extra beds are available.
