General Facilities, Services and Regulations

Pets

Only dogs are allowed with a $20 pet fee. Pet bowls can be provided.

General and Reception Services

Free wifi, free parking, fax/photocopying, daily housekeeping, wake-up services/alarm clock, fully-refundable rooms, allow reserve-now and pay-later, reception invoice provided.

Outdoor Services

Balcony, picnic area, outdoor furniture, mountain views.

Accessibility Services

Facilities for disabled guests

Entertainment & Family Services

Board games/puzzles in the main lobby

Safety & Security

Designated smoking areas, smoke detector, carbon monoxide detector, fire extinguisher, first aid kit, CCTV outside property.
