Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 6 2026.

About this privacy notice

We are Grande Mountain Lodge and this privacy notice is intended for travellers using or considering using our products and services. Your privacy is important to us. In accordance with Alberta privacy laws, we have appointed Ying Zhu as our designated Privacy Officer to oversee our data protection practices and ensure your information is handled with the highest level of integrity.

You place your trust in us by using Grande Mountain Lodge services and we value that trust.

This privacy notice describes how we collect and otherwise process your personal data when you visit our websites, use our mobile apps or buy a travel-related product or service through us. Amongst other things, it tells you what rights you have in relation to your personal data and how you can contact us.

Grande Mountain Lodge offers online travel-related services through its own websites and mobile apps as well as through third-party channels, for example; partners' websites.

This privacy notice applies to any kind of traveller information Grande Mountain Lodge processes through all of our services. It is not the only privacy notice Grande Mountain Lodge maintains to inform you about our processing of personal data. A similar privacy notice is available for Grande Mountain Lodge business partners.

Grande Mountain Lodge amends its privacy notices from time to time and recommends that you visit this notice occasionally to stay informed. If Grande Mountain Lodge makes updates to a privacy notice that could impact persons significantly, it will take steps to inform these persons about such changes before they take effect.

Personal data we collect and process

When you make a trip reservation, you are asked for your first name, last name and email address. Depending on the nature of the trip reservation, we may also need to ask you for your home address, telephone number, payment information, email address and any preferences you might have for your trip (such as accessibility requirements). As part of our mandatory check-in procedure, we also collect a digital photograph of a valid government-issued identification document for every guest to verify identity and maintain accurate lodging records. Digital photographs of government-issued identification are stored on a secure local computer and are accessed only for legitimate identity verification purposes.

It may be that you use our platform to make a trip reservation on behalf of, or that involves other travellers. In this case, you may be required to provide some details about these persons as part of the trip reservation. When you use the platform to give us information about others, it's your responsibility to ensure that each person whose personal data you provide is aware that you're doing so and can understand how Grande Mountain Lodge uses their personal data (as described in this private policy).

Third-Party Service Providers

We may integrate a third-party payment service provider to facilitate electronic payments between you and Grande Mountain Lodge. These service providers share payment information so we can safely administer and handle your trip reservation.

We do not sell your personal information to any third parties.

Purposes of collecting and processing your personal data

We use your personal data for a number of purposes as outlined here:

Trip reservations

First and foremost, we process your personal data to complete and administer your trip reservation – which is essential to provide this service. This includes sending you communications that relate to your trip reservation, such as confirmations (including, where applicable, providing you with a proof of purchase and/or payment), modifications and reminders. In some cases, this may also include processing your personal data to enable online check-in or processing personal data in relation to possible damage deposits. In addition to your contact details (i.e. your email address and telephone number), in order to provide you services, we may also need reservation identifiers and dates to determine the duration of the reservation.

Customer service

We provide our travellers with customer service in English via phone or email, and we're here to help during our working hours from 9AM to 9PM, 7 days a week. Sharing reservation information with our customer service staff is essential to help you when you need us. This includes for example responding to any questions you might have about your trip reservation. To do this, we use personal data such as your reservation details including, for example, the price of your reservation as well as how and when you made the reservation.

Communicating with you

There may be times when we get in touch with you including via email or phone call. Which method we choose depends on the contact information you've previously utilized. We process personal data in communications you and other parties send to us. There may be a number of reasons for this, including:

  • Responding to and handling any support requests made by you.
  • We send you other material related to your trip reservations through a confirmation email, such as how to contact us if you need assistance while you're away and information that we feel might be useful to you to prepare for your trip and get the best experience.

To do this, we use personal data such as your first and last name, email address and reservation details including reservation IDs and chosen locations.

We do not utilize your email address or contact information for marketing purposes, nor do we send unsolicited promotional materials or discount codes.

Customer reviews and other destination-related information

During and after your trip, we might encourage you or your travelling companion to submit a review on a separate review website. This invitation asks for information about the motel experience from the guests' standpoint. The reviews help to inform other travellers about the quality of our motel service, the destination you chose, or any other experiences you choose to share without disclosing your identity. Reviews submitted by travellers are subject to the Terms of Service of the specific website and to automated and other content moderation to verify that reviews conform with their Content standards and guidelines.

Promotion of a safe and trustworthy service and prevention of fraud

We continuously analyse and use certain personal data to prevent and detect online fraud attempts and other illegal or unwanted activities. This is necessary to maintain our platform as a trustworthy environment as well as for the safety of all travellers.

We use personal data for safety and security purposes, including when you report a safety concern, when others do so about you or when we need to identify persons in connection with a user account or reservation. When we do this, we may have to stop or put certain reservations on hold until we finish our assessment. If we have concerns about serious misconduct, we may cancel your upcoming reservations or decline your future reservations via our platform.

For these purposes, we use your contact information and reservation details including cancelled reservations, reviews or other information that you or another person has provided us, including images, video or other media submitted on our platform or to our front desk. While we prioritize guest safety, please note that Grande Mountain Lodge does not utilize CCTV or video surveillance technologies on our premises.

Legal purposes

In certain cases, we may need to reuse your information to handle and resolve legal claims and disputes, address possible regulatory investigations, enforce our reservation Terms of Service, comply with lawful requests from law enforcement, or comply with laws and regulations that apply to Grande Mountain Lodge. We do not collect or process specific documentation regarding service animal certifications beyond what is required to facilitate reasonable accommodation under applicable human rights legislation.

For example, we may be required to process your reservation history, the details of one or more of these reservations and associated payment information.

How we share personal data with third parties

In certain circumstances, we'll share your personal data with third parties. These third parties include Google Gmail. To complete your trip reservation, we need to transfer relevant reservation details to these third party service providers.

Depending on the trip reservation, the details we share can include your name, contact, payment details and any other relevant information (such as check-in/check-out dates) including preferences you specified when you made your trip reservation.

If you have a query about your trip, we may contact you to handle your request. To resolve possible trip related claims or disputes or any other kind of customer service issue, we may provide your contact details and other information about the reservation, claim or dispute. This can include, for example, your email address and a copy of your reservation confirmation to confirm the trip reservation was made or to confirm the reasons for its cancellation.

How we protect personal data

We combine people, processes and technology to protect your personal data and respect your privacy.

This includes, amongst other things, that we:

  • Maintain a comprehensive framework of security policies, procedures and protocols
  • Employ personnel dedicated to cybersecurity and data protection
  • Keep staff alert to security risks through continual security training and awareness activities
  • Use up-to-date security technologies such as encryption and data leakage prevention to help guard against unauthorised data disclosure or destruction
  • In addition to digital safeguards, we maintain strict physical security protocols for all paper registration cards and hard-copy guest records, which are stored in secure, restricted-access areas.
  • Maintain inventories to provide oversight over processes, systems and data assets
  • Use multiple systems for fraud prevention/detection and continuous system monitoring, including for security purposes
  • Maintain and test protocols to respond to reports about possible incidents and data breaches
  • Verify and enhance our security systems, procedures and protocols on a recurring basis
  • Restrict access to both physical filing cabinets and digital identification records to authorized personnel, specifically the Privacy Officer and the Onsite Manager.

Generally, we keep your personal data for as long as is necessary to:

  • Enable you to use our services or to provide our services to you
  • Prevent and detect online fraud attempts and/or other illegal activities
  • Comply with legal obligations such as those from accounting and tax laws
  • Resolve any disputes and legal claims

We typically retain digital ID photos and paper registration cards for approximately six months, after which physical records are destroyed using a professional shredding device and digital files are permanently deleted.

How we treat personal data belonging to minors

Our booking services are not intended for persons under the age of 18 years old. We don't collect personal data about persons below that age (collectively called "minors") unless that data is provided by (and with the consent of) a parent or guardian. The limited circumstances where we might need to collect from parents or guardians the personal data of minors include:

  • As part of a reservation

If we become aware (for example, via a Customer Support request) that we've processed personal data about minors without the valid consent of their parent or guardian, we will delete it.

Your rights

We want you to be in control of how we use your personal data. You can do this in the following ways:

Right Description
Access You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
Correction You can inform us at any time of changes to your personal data and ask us to correct certain personal data we hold about you. We rely on you to make sure that your personal information is complete, accurate and current.
Erasure You can ask us to erase the personal data we hold about you when, for example, it is no longer needed or we have asked for your consent and you later withdraw it.
Restriction In certain situations, you can ask us to block or restrict the processing of the personal data we hold about you and object to particular ways in which we are using your personal data.
Portability In certain situations, you can also ask us to provide you with specific personal data you have given us for possible transmission to a third party.
Withdrawal of consent Where we have collected and processed your personal data on the basis of your consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time, subject to applicable law.
Objection Where we process your personal data based on legitimate interest or the public interest, you have the right to object to that use of your personal data at any time, subject to applicable law.
