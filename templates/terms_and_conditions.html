About These Terms

Grande Mountain Lodge is a motel with 52 rooms in total, of which 38 are currently in operation. We welcome guests travelling to Grande Cache and the surrounding area for work, recreation, and time in the mountains.

These Terms are intended to set out, in plain language, what guests can expect from us and what we ask of guests during the reservation process and during their stay. They apply to direct reservations made through our website, by telephone, or in person at the lodge, unless a separate agreement applies to a particular booking.

Reservations

You may make a reservation through our website, by calling us at 780-827-2007, by email at reception@grandemountainlodge.com, in person at the front desk, or through third-party booking websites such as Booking.com or Expedia.

A reservation is considered confirmed once we have accepted it and you have received confirmation. For online bookings made through our website, confirmation is usually sent by email after the reservation is recorded. For telephone bookings, confirmation may be given verbally at the time of the call. For walk-in bookings, confirmation occurs when the reservation is accepted at the front desk.

Please review your confirmation carefully, including dates, room type, number of guests, and contact information. If anything appears incorrect, contact us as soon as possible so we can review it with you.

All reservations are subject to room availability at the time of booking. If we need clarification about a reservation, we may contact you using the phone number or email address you provided.

We may decline or cancel a reservation if we reasonably believe it cannot be honoured as requested, if required information is missing or inaccurate, or if there is a concern related to safety, occupancy limits, or compliance with these Terms.

Rates and Payment

Rates are generally quoted per room, per night unless we tell you otherwise. Applicable taxes, including GST and the Alberta Tourism Levy where applicable, are additional unless a rate is expressly stated as inclusive.

Please review the rate, taxes, and total shown during the booking process before confirming your reservation. If you have questions about how a rate was calculated, contact us before your arrival.

When you reserve through our website, no payment is collected online at the time of booking. Payment for your stay is made in person at the lodge.

At check-in, government-issued photo identification is required, and a credit card must be presented as required by the lodge.

Accepted Payment Methods: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Interac Debit, Cash, and E-Transfers. Cheques are not accepted.

Cancellations and Changes

There is no cancellation fee. If your plans change and you need to cancel, please contact us as soon as you can so the room may be made available to other guests.

You may cancel by phone at 780-827-2007 or by email at reception@grandemountainlodge.com. When contacting us, please provide your name, stay dates, and confirmation number if you have one.

If you need to change your dates or room type, contact us and we will do our best to help, subject to availability.

There is no no-show fee. If you know you will not be arriving, please let us know as soon as possible. A reservation that is not used may be released according to our normal reservation procedures so that the room can be offered to another guest.

If you plan to depart earlier than originally booked, please speak with the front desk so your reservation can be reviewed with you.

Check-In and Check-Out

Check-in begins at 3:00 PM. Regular check-in is available until 9:00 PM. All times are local to Grande Cache, Alberta.

At check-in, each guest must present valid government-issued photo identification and a credit card as required by the lodge. If you expect to arrive outside our regular check-in hours, please contact us in advance so we can note your reservation accordingly. Early check-in may be possible depending on availability; contact us ahead of time if you require an earlier arrival.

Check-out is by 11:00 AM. Late check-out may be available depending on occupancy and staffing; please contact us in advance if you need a later departure.

Occupancy and Guests

The minimum age to book and register for a room is 18 years.

Maximum occupancy is four persons per room. For rooms that accommodate four guests, one adult may stay with up to three minors, or two adults may stay with up to two minors. Minors under 18 must stay with an adult.

All guests staying overnight must be registered at the front desk. Overnight visitors who are not registered are not permitted.

Guests must not exceed the stated occupancy limit for the room they have booked.

Guest Conduct and Use of the Property

We ask that all guests treat other guests, lodge staff, and the property with respect. Guests are expected to take reasonable care of their room and common areas, follow reasonable instructions from lodge staff, and avoid behaviour that creates a significant disturbance, safety concern, or damage to the property or its contents.

Smoking and non-smoking rooms are available. Designated smoking areas are provided on the property. Guests who book a non-smoking room should not smoke in that room. No additional fees are charged for smoking violations; however, guests may be asked to move to an appropriate room or area where smoking is permitted.

If a guest’s behaviour seriously disrupts other guests or affects the safe operation of the lodge, we may take reasonable steps in response, which may include asking the guest to leave in accordance with applicable law.

Pets

Pets are welcome at Grande Mountain Lodge. We allow dogs only. A pet fee of $20 per pet applies. Service animals are welcome and are exempt from pet fees.

There are no specific size or breed restrictions. Guests are responsible for supervising their pets at all times and for ensuring that pets do not disturb other guests or damage lodge property. Pet bowls may be available upon request.

Housekeeping and Room Access

Housekeeping may be arranged on a daily, weekly, or monthly basis depending on the length and nature of your stay. If you have a preference for how often your room is serviced, please let us know when you check in or contact the front desk during your stay.

From time to time, lodge staff may need to enter a guest room for cleaning, maintenance, repairs, safety checks, or emergencies. We will attempt to respect guest privacy and, where practical, provide notice before entering a room for non-emergency reasons.

Parking

Free on-site parking is available for registered guests during their stay.

Guest Belongings and Responsibility

Guests are responsible for their personal belongings during their stay. Please take reasonable precautions with valuables and personal items, especially when leaving your room.

If you believe you have left something behind after check-out, contact the lodge as soon as possible. While we will make reasonable efforts to help locate lost items, we cannot guarantee that lost property will be found or retained indefinitely.
